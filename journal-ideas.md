## How I work - the yearly series

Direction (2026-08-14): "How I work" becomes a journal series I re-reflect on every year - `how-i-work-2026` and so on. The old ones stay up; the stack is its own evidence (someone reading two or three years of them sees a person who actually revisits their methods).

- `/how-i-work` the page LISTS the series (latest featured, previous years under it) - it does not redirect. The stable URL keeps working for resumes and target notes; the list shows the pattern.
- The list derives from `journal.json` (a `series` key or a slug convention) - never hand-copied onto the page.
- Audience tension to keep deliberate: the page version is for recruiters mid-application; the yearly reflection is partly for me. Don't let one document try to be both or the candor gets sanded off.
