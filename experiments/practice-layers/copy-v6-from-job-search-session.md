## Voice rule (as sent)

No "I learned X." Present-tense capability statements. Paragraphs describe the work / the capability, not autobiography. 8 panels + closing.

## The eight panels (as sent)

```
01 — Visual thinking, critical thinking, and the tools to make things.
Critical thinking. Judgment about what's worth making. Comfort with ambiguity. Storytelling. Enough technical foundation to build the thing, not just picture it.
ART + TECHNICAL EDUCATION
visual thinking · critique · theory · technical craft

02 — Design and code as one job.
Design decisions get argued out in the browser as much as in the sketch. The build teaches the design what's possible; the design teaches the build what matters. Implementation isn't the thing that happens after design — it's part of the thing.
WEB DESIGN + DEVELOPMENT
HTML · CSS · JavaScript · SVG · animation · accessibility

03 — Good work has to survive contact with a team.
Building code other people can live with. Meeting real production constraints. Working across teams without the work getting lost in handoff.
FRONT-END ENGINEERING
production · performance · QA · systems · cross-functional teams

04 — Building when the answer isn't known yet.
Moving from unclear problem to something concrete enough to test. Comfort with everything moving at once. Deciding what to build, and what not to.
EARLY-STAGE PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT
UX · UI · prototyping · research · testing · shipping

05 — The thing you're asked to fix isn't always the problem.
Diagnosing the actual problem underneath the stated one. Reading team dynamics. Working across design, engineering, and product without allegiance to any of them.
PRODUCT + TECHNICAL CONSULTING
discovery · facilitation · strategy · systems thinking · alignment

06 — Holding the whole product in view.
The user, the business, the system, the interface, the technology — and the tradeoffs between them. Not just can we build it? but what are we trying to accomplish?
PRODUCT DESIGN
research · interaction · UI · design systems · product strategy

07 — Making practice into a system other people can use.
Turning years of instinct into something another person can pick up. Precision about what I actually know and don't. Building feedback loops that produce outcomes, not just information.
DESIGN EDUCATION + SYSTEMS
curriculum · teaching · feedback · assessment · mentorship

08 — AI on top of the layers, not instead of them.
Shortens the loop between thinking and making. The value isn't doing more — it's knowing what to accelerate, what to question, and what shouldn't be handed over.
AI + AGENTIC WORKFLOWS
agents · LLMs · rapid prototyping · workflow design · experimentation
```

New in this set, compared with what the component holds today: a fourth part per layer (a row of skill words under the name), and layer 07 renamed from "Education systems + teaching" to "Design education + systems".
