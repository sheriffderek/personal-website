## Bio - full (third person)

Derek Wood is a designer, developer and educator based in Los Angeles. He originally studied printmaking and painting, then got serious about web design in 2011. He started out freelancing and went on to work across agencies, consulting, and startups, wearing all the hats along the way before moving into product design.

Frustrated by siloed teams and the gap between visual design and development, he founded Perpetual Education in 2019 and developed its flagship program, Design for the Web: a holistic digital product design course where students learn to design and build web applications while exploring every role involved in the process.

Derek also volunteers, holds open office hours, and consults on product design and design systems. Outside of work, he makes music and might be skateboarding again for the first time since the late '90s.

## Form lessons

- Aim ~15% under character limits - form counters can read higher than your editor's.
- Check hashtags and years on forms; they're often left over from last year (IAC27's form said #IAC26).
- Have headshot, bio and tagline ready before opening the form - the extras are what make deadlines stressful.
